How it works

Getting started takes one meeting and a signature.

  1. We talk

    A free 30-minute conversation, at the office or over the phone. You tell us about the business and what is bothering you. We tell you honestly what you need and what you do not.

  2. You get a fixed quote

    One monthly figure covering everything we have agreed. No hourly rates, no surprise invoice for a phone call. If your business changes, we revisit the fee together.

  3. We take it from there

    We write to your previous accountant, register as your agent with HMRC, set up your software if you need it, and give you a simple list of what we need from you and when.

Deadlines we keep an eye on for you

  • 31 January — Self Assessment return and payment
  • Quarterly — VAT returns under Making Tax Digital
  • 9 months after year end — Company accounts to Companies House
  • 12 months after year end — Corporation tax return
  • Monthly — PAYE and pension submissions
  • Annually — Confirmation statement

We send reminders well before each one and chase you if we have to. Nobody has paid a late filing penalty on our watch that they could have avoided.

Why a chartered firm

Anyone can call themselves an accountant. Chartered means something.

In the UK the word "accountant" is not protected. A chartered accountant has passed professional exams, is bound by a code of ethics, carries professional indemnity insurance, and is regulated by a professional body that can take action if things go wrong.

For you that means someone who is accountable, who keeps their knowledge current every year, and who has to tell you if you are about to do something unwise. It is the difference between a bookkeeper who files what you give them and an adviser who asks why.

  • Regulated practice with professional indemnity cover
  • Ongoing training on every Budget and HMRC change
  • Registered agent with HMRC, so we deal with them on your behalf
