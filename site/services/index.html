Fees

How we charge

Fixed monthly fees, agreed before any work starts and paid by direct debit. The fee depends on the volume of transactions, whether you are VAT registered, how many people are on the payroll, and how much of the bookkeeping you want to do yourself.

As a rough guide, a sole trader with tidy records usually pays less than a contractor's limited company, which in turn pays less than a VAT-registered business with staff. We will give you an exact figure after the first meeting.
