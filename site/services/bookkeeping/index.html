Books that are up to date, not a shoebox in December.

Good bookkeeping is the difference between knowing how the business is doing and finding out at the year end. We either keep the books for you or set you up on software and check them each month, whichever you prefer. Either way you get figures you can actually make decisions on.

Two ways to work

We do it. You forward invoices and receipts to a capture app, your bank feeds in automatically, and we reconcile everything monthly. You get a short summary each month and can log in at any time to see where things stand. This suits busy owners who would rather be doing anything else.

You do it, we check it. We set up the software, chart of accounts and bank feed, show you or a member of staff how to code transactions and raise invoices, and then review the books each month or quarter to pick up anything miscoded before it reaches the VAT return. Cheaper, and fine if someone in the business has an hour a week.

Software

We work day to day with Xero, QuickBooks Online and FreeAgent, and can support Sage if you are already on it. For most trades and service businesses Xero or FreeAgent does everything needed. We are not tied to one provider and will recommend the one that suits your bank, your invoicing habits and your budget. Some business bank accounts include FreeAgent free of charge, which is worth knowing before you pay for a subscription.

What good books give you

  • A VAT return that takes an hour instead of a weekend
  • Knowing who owes you money and chasing it before it gets old
  • A realistic tax estimate months before the bill, so the money is put aside
  • Management figures you can show a lender, a landlord or a buyer
  • A year end with no surprises, because we have seen every month

If you are behind, that is not unusual and not a problem. We regularly take on businesses that are a year or more out of date, get the backlog done in one go, and then keep them current.

Common questions

Which software do you recommend?

For a sole trader or small limited company, FreeAgent or Xero. For a retail or stock-heavy business, QuickBooks or Xero with an inventory add-on. We will look at how you work before suggesting one, and we do not take commission that would bias the advice.

Do I need a bookkeeper and an accountant?

Not two separate people, no. Bookkeeping is the monthly recording; accounting is the year end, tax and advice built on top of it. Having both done by one firm means nothing falls between the two.

Can you work with my existing bookkeeper?

Yes. Several clients have an in-house or freelance bookkeeper and we review their work quarterly and do the year end. We are happy to work that way.

How much paperwork do I need to keep?

Digital copies are fine for HMRC. Photograph receipts into the software as you go and you can throw the paper away. Keep records for six years for a company and five years after the filing deadline for a sole trader.
