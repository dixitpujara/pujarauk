Payroll run on time, every time, without you thinking about it.

Paying staff correctly is one of the few things a business cannot get wrong twice. We run payroll for employers from one director to a few dozen staff, handle every HMRC and pension submission, and send payslips out before payday.

What is involved

Every time you pay someone, HMRC has to be told on or before the day through a Full Payment Submission under Real Time Information. Pension contributions have to be calculated and paid to the scheme. Starters need P45s processing, leavers need them issuing, and statutory sick, maternity and paternity pay have their own rules. At the end of the tax year there are P60s for every employee by 31 May and P11Ds for benefits by 6 July. Miss a monthly submission and HMRC's penalties start automatically.

None of it is difficult in isolation. It is the relentlessness that catches small employers out, especially when the person who normally does it is on holiday.

How we run it

You tell us hours, overtime, new starters and leavers by an agreed date each month, by email or through a simple online portal. We process the payroll, send you a summary to approve, then issue password-protected payslips to staff by email, submit the FPS to HMRC, calculate what you owe in PAYE and National Insurance, and upload pension contributions to your provider. You get a one-page report showing what to pay and to whom.

Directors' payroll

If you are the only employee of your own company you still need a PAYE scheme to pay yourself a salary, and the right salary level is a tax planning decision. We set up the scheme, run it annually or monthly as suits, and make sure the figures tie up with the company accounts and your personal tax return.

Workplace pensions

Every employer must assess staff for automatic enrolment and put eligible workers into a qualifying scheme. We handle the assessment each pay period, the enrolment letters, the opt-outs, and the declaration of compliance to The Pensions Regulator, which is required within five months of your duties start date and then every three years at re-enrolment. If you do not have a scheme yet we will help you choose one.

Employment Allowance. Most small employers can reduce their employer National Insurance bill by up to £10,500 a year. It is not applied automatically; it has to be claimed through the payroll, and we check every client is claiming it where eligible.

Common questions

How much does payroll cost?

A fixed monthly fee based on how many people are paid and how often. A single-director payroll is a small addition to an accounts package; a monthly payroll for ten staff is more. We will quote exactly once we know the numbers.

Can you take over mid-year?

Yes. We need the year-to-date figures from your current software or provider, which are straightforward to export, and we can usually be running the next payroll within a week.

What about CIS subcontractors?

CIS is separate from payroll but we handle it too: verifying subcontractors with HMRC, applying the right deduction, issuing statements and filing the monthly CIS return by the 19th.

Do I need to give staff a pension if I only have one employee?

If they are aged 22 to state pension age and earn over £10,000 a year, yes. Directors without an employment contract are usually exempt, but the business still has to tell The Pensions Regulator that.
