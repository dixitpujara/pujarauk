What you can expect from us

We would rather set expectations now than have you find out later.

  • A reply the same working day. Not always a full answer, but you will know we have seen it and when you will hear back.
  • Plain English. If we have to use a technical term we will explain it. You should never leave a meeting unsure what was decided.
  • Fees agreed in advance. Monthly, fixed, and reviewed once a year or when your business changes. We do not charge for phone calls or quick emails.
  • Honesty about what you do not need. Plenty of sole traders do not need a limited company. Plenty of businesses do not need monthly management accounts. We will say so.
  • Deadlines managed, not just met. You will hear from us weeks before anything is due, with a short list of what we need.
