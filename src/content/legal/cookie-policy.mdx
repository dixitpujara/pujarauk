## The short version

This website sets no cookies of its own. We use a cookieless analytics service that counts visits in aggregate, and we store two small items in your browser so that the site works the way you expect. Nothing on this site is used for advertising, remarketing or social media tracking.

## The law

The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, govern when a website may store information on your device or read information from it, and they require consent for most storage that is not strictly necessary, with a limited exception for low-risk statistical measurement.

## How we measure visits

We use Plausible Analytics, which:

- sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device
- does not record your IP address or any persistent identifier
- does not track you across other websites
- reports only aggregate figures such as page views, referrers, countries and device types
- acts as our processor under a written contract

We rely on the statistical-purposes exception in PECR for this measurement, because no information is stored on your device and the data cannot be used to identify you. [LEGAL CHECK]

If we ever add Google Analytics or any advertising tag, we will add a consent banner where nothing fires before you opt in, "Reject all" is as prominent as "Accept all", and you can change your choice at any time. We have no plans to do so.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. The instructions are different for each browser:

- Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Third-party cookies and Site settings
- Safari: Preferences, Privacy, Manage Website Data
- Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data
- Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions

Blocking storage for this site will not stop it working, apart from the booking widget and the unsent-draft feature.
