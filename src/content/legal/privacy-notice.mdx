## What personal data we collect

### Enquiry form

When you send an enquiry through this website we collect:

- your name
- your email address
- your phone number, if you choose to give it
- the type of enquirer you are (accountancy practice, business or individual)
- the service you are interested in
- your message
- whether you have ticked the separate marketing opt-in box

Please do not send us National Insurance numbers, Unique Taxpayer References, bank details, passwords or documents through the website. The form is for an initial enquiry only. Once we are working together we will give you a secure way to share documents.

### Booking a consultation

If you book a call through the booking widget on our Contact page, the booking provider [CONFIRM: tool] collects your name, email address, the time you choose and any notes you add. The provider's own privacy notice also applies to that booking.

### Clients and prospective clients

If you become a client we hold the information needed to carry out the engagement, such as your contact details, business details, financial records, correspondence and the identification information we are required to collect before starting work.

### Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a cookieless, aggregate-only analytics service. It does not set cookies, does not build a profile of you and does not store your IP address. We see page views, referrers, countries and device types in aggregate only. See our Cookie Policy for details and for how to opt out.

### Email and phone

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we keep a record of the correspondence so that we can respond and, where relevant, keep a record of the engagement.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access)
- ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (rectification)
- ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances (erasure)
- ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances (restriction)
- receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format and have it moved to another provider (portability)
- object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time (objection)
- withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis we rely on

We do not make decisions about you using automated decision-making or profiling.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@pujaraandco.co.uk. We will respond within one month, and will tell you if we need longer for a complex request. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.
