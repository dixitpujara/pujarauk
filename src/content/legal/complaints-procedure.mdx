## Our commitment

We want every client to be satisfied with the work we do and the way we do it. If something has gone wrong, tell us. We will treat your complaint seriously, deal with it promptly and use it to improve.

## How to complain

You can complain in whichever way suits you:

- Email: hello@pujaraandco.co.uk, with "Complaint" in the subject line
- Post: Pujara & Co, Chartered Accountants, A-309, Privilon, Behind Iskcon Temple, Ambli Road, Satellite, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380058, India
- Phone: +91 90168 75077 or +91 73833 75077, during UK working hours

It helps if you tell us what happened, when, who you dealt with, and what you would like us to do to put it right. Please do not include bank details or identity documents in your first message; we will ask for anything we need through a secure channel.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement.** We will acknowledge your complaint in writing within 3 UK working days and tell you who is handling it.
2. **Investigation.** The person handling your complaint will review the file, speak to the people involved and, where helpful, speak to you.
3. **Response.** We will send you a full written response within 20 UK working days of receiving the complaint. If we cannot meet that date, for example because we are waiting for information, we will write to explain why and give a new date.
4. **Outcome.** The response will set out what we found, whether we uphold the complaint in whole or in part, and what we propose to do. That may include an apology, correcting the work, or a fee adjustment.

## Escalation to the principal

If you are unhappy with the response, ask for the complaint to be referred to Mitul Pujara, FCA (ICAI), the principal of the firm. He will review the complaint afresh and reply within 10 UK working days. If he handled the original complaint, he will review it together with an independent senior member of the team.

## Taking it further

If you remain dissatisfied after the principal's review, you may refer the matter to our professional body. ## Complaints about personal data

If your complaint is about how we have handled your personal data, you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or on 0303 123 1113. If you are in the EU or EEA, you can contact the supervisory authority in your member state. Our Privacy Notice has more detail.
