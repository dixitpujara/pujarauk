## Our commitment

Pujara & Co wants everyone to be able to use this website. We have built it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 at Level AA, and we test against that standard.

## What we have done

- Pages use semantic HTML with one main heading, a logical heading order, landmarks and lists, so that screen readers can navigate them.
- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone. A "skip to content" link appears at the top of each page, and the element with focus is always visible.
- Text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 against its background, and interactive elements meet the 3 to 1 ratio.
- Text can be enlarged to 200 percent without losing content or function, and pages work from a screen width of 320 pixels.
- Animations are minimal and are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
- Every form field has a visible label. Errors are described in text next to the field and announced to assistive technology.
- Images that carry meaning have text alternatives. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
- Links make sense out of context, and the site does not rely on colour alone to convey information.

## Tell us about a problem

If you find something you cannot use, or you need information in a different format, email hello@pujaraandco.co.uk and tell us the page address and what went wrong. We will reply within 5 UK working days and do our best to fix the problem quickly.

## The legal position

Pujara & Co is not a UK public sector body, so the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 do not apply to this site. We do have duties under the Equality Act 2010 not to discriminate against disabled people, and we take those duties seriously. The European Accessibility Act has applied to listed consumer services since 28 June 2025, and although an accountancy enquiry site is unlikely to be a listed service, meeting WCAG 2.2 AA puts the question beyond doubt.
